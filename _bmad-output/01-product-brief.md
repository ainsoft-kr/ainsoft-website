# 01 Product Brief

Updated: 2026-03-12

## Product

Corporate website for the company.

## Goal

Build a web-only company homepage that clearly explains the company, introduces products, supports inquiry conversion, and works responsively across mobile, tablet, and desktop.

## Current Direction

- Web only
- Flutter web
- Responsive layout required
- `responsive_framework` adopted

## Primary Business Goals

1. Present the company professionally
2. Explain products clearly
3. Show solution value, not only product features
4. Convert visitors into inquiries or demo/contact leads

## Primary Users

1. Potential customers
2. Business partners
3. Prospective employees
4. General visitors checking company credibility

## MVP Scope

Top navigation for first release:

1. Home
2. About
3. Products
4. Contact

Recommended next page after MVP:

5. Solutions

## Core CTA

- Contact us
- Request consultation
- Request demo

## Content Priorities

1. Company credibility
2. Product understanding
3. Business use cases
4. Easy contact path

## Constraints

- No iOS app target
- No Android app target
- Website should be responsive from the start
