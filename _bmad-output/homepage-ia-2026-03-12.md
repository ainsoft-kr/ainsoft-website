## Page Purpose Notes

### Home

- Main value proposition
- Key product summary
- Core CTA
- Trust elements

### About

- Company intro
- Mission / vision
- Team or company strengths
- History or milestones

### Products

- Product overview
- Feature highlights
- Product comparison or lineup
- Demo / inquiry CTA

### Solutions

- Industry or use-case based grouping
- Problem to solution mapping

### Contact

- Inquiry form
- Email / phone / address
- Map or business info
