# Homepage Sections Draft

Updated: 2026-03-12

## Recommended Top Navigation

1. Home
2. About
3. Products
4. Solutions
5. Contact

## 1. Home

Recommended sections:

1. Hero
2. Core value proposition
3. Product highlights
4. Solutions by use case
5. Why us
6. Customer or partner logos
7. CTA banner
8. Footer

### Home section notes

- Hero: short headline, sub copy, primary CTA, secondary CTA
- Core value proposition: what the company does in one screen
- Product highlights: 3 to 5 cards
- Solutions by use case: industry or problem-based blocks
- Why us: strengths, speed, reliability, technology, support
- CTA banner: inquiry, demo, consultation

## 2. About

Recommended sections:

1. Company introduction
2. Mission and vision
3. Key strengths
4. Company history or milestones
5. Leadership or team
6. Certifications or awards

### About section notes

- Keep this page corporate and trust-oriented
- If team exposure is weak, replace leadership section with company capabilities

## 3. Products

Recommended sections:

1. Product overview
2. Product lineup
3. Main features
4. Product detail cards
5. Architecture or workflow
6. Demo / inquiry CTA

### Products section notes

- If there is only one product, use product overview plus feature detail sections
- If there are multiple products, use a lineup grid and drill-down pattern

## 4. Solutions

Recommended sections:

1. Problems we solve
2. Industry-based solutions
3. Use-case flow
4. Expected outcomes
5. Case teaser
6. Consultation CTA

### Solutions section notes

- This page should translate product features into business outcomes
- Strong for B2B conversion

## 5. Contact

Recommended sections:

1. Contact hero
2. Inquiry form
3. Company contact information
4. Location or map
5. FAQ shortcut

### Contact section notes

- Keep form short
- Support both direct inquiry and sales/demo request
